CMPUT 350 Project 1b

Game
Galaga is started by adding one Galaga object to the engine. That object creates the stars,
the player, and the enemies.

State flow
Waiting: press 5 to add a credit, press 1 to spend one credit and start.
Startup: the level number is shown and the player ship appears.
Waves: five waves of eight ships fly in on cubic Bezier paths.
Gameplay: ships in formation sway together. Two ships are chosen to dive, shoot, and return.
         Clearing the formation starts the next level at Startup.
         A death waits until flying ships are home, then the player respawns.
         At 0 lives the state becomes GameOver, then returns to Waiting.

Design
Drawing and collision shapes are in local space around (0, 0). The engine adds each object's
location and rotation. Bullets collide as the line traveled since the previous frame, and a hit
is drawn as a circle on the contact point. Enemy 1 and Enemy 2 have 1 health. Enemy 3 has 2
health and changes color after the first hit. A ship that is flying scores more than the same
ship sitting in formation. Movement and firing use notification strings, so A and Left both
send MoveLeft.
